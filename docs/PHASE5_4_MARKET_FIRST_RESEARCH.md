# Phase 5.4 — Market-First Research

## Why
Research and compare screens previously gave Market, Infrastructure, Legal and Macro similar visual weight. For real-estate decision work, Market should lead while other modules explain context.

## Hierarchy
1. Subject hero with market-data coverage
2. Market Performance / Market Comparison Matrix
3. Notable updates or projects to explore
4. Supporting context: Infrastructure, Legal and compact Macro
5. Research Brief / data gaps

## Market comparison
- Project / Developer / Region comparison prioritizes project count, selling/ongoing projects, price coverage, sales coverage, absorption coverage and latest sourced market observations.
- Compatible region/segment benchmarks are shown separately.
- Missing values remain blank.

## Guardrails
- no inferred ASP, sales or absorption
- no synthetic score or better/worse ranking
- Infrastructure and Legal remain context, not a substitute for Market evidence
- Macro remains common context, not project-specific causality
- canonical data and Macro production are unchanged
