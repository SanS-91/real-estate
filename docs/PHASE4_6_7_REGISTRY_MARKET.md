# Phase 4.6 + 4.7 — Assisted Registry Updates & Market Coverage

## Phase 4.6
Legal and Infrastructure use a weekly assisted source watch. It only creates candidate/review artifacts and has no write permission to canonical registries or frontend data. Changed source pages, unavailable sources and new keyword-matched official links are reviewed before a curated registry update. Infrastructure schedule revisions are appended and the previous current target is superseded rather than erased.

## Phase 4.7
Curated project coverage expands from 8 to 12 projects and developer coverage from 4 to 9. New coverage includes Vinhomes Grand Park, The 9 Stellars, Celesta Gold and Essensia Parkway. Quantitative observations remain separate: missing ASP, sales, supply or absorption values stay blank rather than being inferred.

## Safety
- retain last good data on source failure
- no automatic business-data publication from the registry watch
- first-party source required for every project master
- no synthetic market history
- coverage QA validates project/developer breadth and reference integrity
