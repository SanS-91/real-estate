# Phase 5.2 — Research Brief / Decision Summary

Phase 5.2 turns the Research workspace into a deterministic brief for one subject or a same-type comparison.

## Brief sections
- Scope / data coverage
- Latest source-backed evidence and canonical links
- Shared context for comparison mode
- Explicit data gaps

## Output actions
- Copy brief as plain text with the shareable Research URL
- Print / Save PDF through the browser print dialog

## Guardrails
- demo-source articles/events are excluded from Research activity
- missing ASP, sales and absorption remain missing
- no synthetic score or better/worse ranking
- legal-topic overlap is research relevance only, not legal applicability
- Macro remains context, not project-specific causality
- canonical data and Macro production are not mutated
